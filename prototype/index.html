1 · Capture

Android Chrome is the reference platform: ImageCapture.takePhoto() returns a full sensor-resolution still in-page, and the optics can be locked so a device calibration profile stays valid for the whole session.

Holding still and tapping the shutter is the hard part. Let the phone take the shot itself the moment it settles — brace against a railing or wall and just wait.

      4b · AR measure & depth (Android)
      

        

          On Android, WebXR hit-test gives marker-free point-to-point measurement, and the ARCore
          depth stream measures how far a feature actually stands off the calibrated plane — which is
          the dominant source of silent error in single-image metrology.
