      4b · AR cross-check (optional, Android)
      

        

          What this is for. Everything else in Plumb measures on the calibrated plane —
          the flat face you photographed. It cannot tell how far a cornice, sill or pier steps
          out of that plane, and that off-plane depth is the largest silent error in
          single-image metrology. This tool uses your phone's AR sensors to measure that depth
          directly, so you can record it instead of guessing.
        

        

          You do not need this for a normal capture. Skip it unless you are checking how far a
          feature stands proud of the wall.
        

        

          It takes over the whole screen. That is how WebXR works — Android hands the display
          to the AR session, so Plumb's own controls disappear until you leave it. You are not stuck:
          
1 · Move the phone slowly so it finds surfaces.
          
2 · Tap a surface to drop a point. Tap a second point and Plumb reports the distance.
          
3 · Press the system Back gesture, or the × Android draws in the corner,
          to come back here. Your points are kept.
